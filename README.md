# TAC

This is like a fridge where you can keep all your travel memories. You can stick magnets add postcards and put up photos from your trips. Then you can share all these things with the people who really care about where you went.

## What TAC is

TAC is a mobile app that works like the fridge door at your home. You know, the one that is covered in magnets from places you visited postcards from friends and photos held up by a magnet from some market in a city you do not remember well.

TAC brings all this to your phone. You can build your fridge with memories of your travels. You can add photos and virtual magnets. Then share them with your friends or family so they can see where you have been.

## How TAC works

You create a fridge in TAC. Then you add memories to it. Photos, postcards and magnets. You organize all these things around the trips you take. Then you can share your fridge with whoever you want. They can see your collection as it grows trip by trip.

That is it. There is no algorithm, no feed, no pressure to post things. It is a place to keep and share what you actually brought back from your trips.

## Install TAC

You can download the version of TAC from the Releases page.

## Requirements

- **minSdk:** 35 (Android 15+)
- **Permissions:**
  - `CAMERA` — to take photos directly in the app
  - `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` — to pick photos from your gallery

## Platform support for TAC

- TAC is available on Android now.

- It is not available on iOS yet.

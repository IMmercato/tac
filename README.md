# TAC

Your digital fridge for travel memories. Stick magnets, postcards, and photos from your trips — and share them with the people who actually care where you went.

## What it is

TAC is a mobile app that works like the fridge door at home. You know the one — covered in magnets from places you visited, postcards from friends, photos held up by a magnet from a market in some city you barely remember.

TAC brings that to your phone. You build a fridge of your own travels, add memories in the form of photos and virtual magnets, and share it with friends or family so they can actually see where you've been.

## Why

Solo travel is great. But you always come back home having forgotten to bring something for your brother, or never quite finding the right magnet for your mom's fridge. You have hundreds of photos on your phone that nobody else sees.

TAC gives you a space to put those memories in a way that feels personal — not just another photo dump in a group chat. Your fridge, your trips, your stuff. People can follow along or check in whenever.

## How it works

You create a fridge. You add memories to it — photos, postcards, magnets — organized around the trips you take. You share your fridge with whoever you want. They can see your collection as it grows, trip by trip.

That's it. No algorithm, no feed, no pressure to post. Just a place to keep and share what you actually brought back.

## Requirements

- **minSdk:** 35 (Android 15+)
- **Permissions:**
    - `CAMERA` — to take photos directly in the app
    - `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` — to pick photos from your gallery
